Delete your Jade Rush data

Jade Rush has no accounts and no servers that store your data. Everything the app keeps is on your phone, so you are always in control of it.

Remove Facebook data

  1. In Jade Rush, open Online → Play with Facebook friends and tap Sign out. This removes your cached name and picture from the app.
  2. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find Jade Rush and tap Remove. Facebook will stop sharing your information with the app.

Remove game progress

Open Android Settings → Apps → Jade Rush → Storage and tap Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

Advertising data

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Change your consent in Jade Rush under Settings → Ad privacy choices, and reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
