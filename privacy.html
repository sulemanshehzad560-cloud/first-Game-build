Jade Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Jade Rush ("the game") is a Mahjong tile-matching game for Android, iPhone and the web. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels cleared, stars, best scores, chosen tile set, sound and vibration settings) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us, and we do not run a server or create accounts.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android and iPhone apps show ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

On iPhone, the app asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is used. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised using the IDFA. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Facebook sign-in (optional)

If you choose "Continue with Facebook", Facebook shares your name, profile picture, Facebook app-scoped user ID and the list of your Facebook friends who also use Jade Rush and have allowed it. The app uses this only on your device, to show your friends and whether they are online. It is not sent to us or stored on any server we run, and nothing is posted to Facebook. Your app-scoped ID is part of the address your device uses to receive game invites (see Online duels below). Sign out at any time from the Friends screen, and remove the app from your Facebook account under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites. Facebook's handling of this data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

Online duels

When you play an online duel, your device connects directly to your opponent's device (peer to peer) using a temporary room code, through the public PeerJS signalling service. Only game moves and emotes are exchanged. Your IP address is visible to the signalling service and to your opponent's device, as with any direct connection. Nothing is stored after the match ends.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Because your progress is stored only on your device, you can delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. See the data deletion instructions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
